### HUD status readouts and alerts

<a id="hud-and-status-readouts"></a>

Reworks the on-screen status layer with new alert widgets, resource bars, ammo counters, job and antag status icons, and richer speech bubbles.

**rmc14**

*loop-change · entangled · high confidence · 8 forks carry it*

Everyone gets a persistent on-screen compass arrow that points toward whoever matters to them: marines track their squad leader (or the captain, the landing zone, the corporate liaison), while xenos track the queen, hive leaders, tunnels and resin markers, with the arrow swinging as you move. A shared tactical map opens from a screen button, showing live blips for your side over the station layout, and leaders can draw coloured lines and drop text labels on it that everyone on their side then sees. Command staff sit at an Overwatch console listing every marine in a squad with their status (conscious, unconscious, dead, SSD), role, location, distance from the squad leader and a saved comment, from which they can broadcast squad-wide messages, set squad objectives, hide or promote people, watch through helmet cameras, and call in supply drops or orbital bombardment. Medics see floating health icons over bodies that distinguish "still defibbable", "nearly too late" and "permanently gone", plus a holocard tag they can set on a patient to flag their treatment status to the next doctor. Senior officers can award named medals with written citations, and anyone can recommend a crewmate for one, with the recommendations queueing up for approval on a medals panel.

- **Take** — It turns "where is everyone and how bad off are they" from radio guesswork into a readable shared picture, which is exactly what command and medical roles lack and the single biggest lever on coordinated play.
- **Skip** — Almost none of it stands alone: the trackers, Overwatch console and medical icons are readouts for RMC-14's squad roster, hive structure and its multi-stage defib window, so grafting the UI without those underlying systems leaves widgets with nothing to display.
- **Touches** — command, medical, security, antagonist, all-players
- **Needs** — [squads-ranks-and-skills](jobs.md#squads-ranks-and-skills), [xenomorph-hive](antag.md#xenomorph-hive), [resuscitation-and-life-support](medical.md#resuscitation-and-life-support)
- **Verified** — supported. Every specific in the gameplay text checks out against the raw files. alerts.yml contains the full tracker family: HiveTracker/LeaderTracker/TunnelTracker/HiveCoreTracker/ResinMarkerTracker for xenos, and a long SquadTracker* list (per-squad leaders, CommandingOfficer, ExecutiveOfficer, ChiefMilitaryPolice, LandingZone, CorporateLiaison) for marines, each with directional trackon-north/south/etc. sprite states matching a swinging compass arrow. overwatch.ftl has every named feature: Name/Role/State(conscious/unconscious/dead/SSD)/Location/SL Dist. columns, COMMENT + Save Comment, message-squad, squad-objectives, supply-drop, orbital-bombardment (warhead/crate/cooldown strings), hide/show-marine, promote-squad-leader, and a NO CAMERA string implying camera viewing. tacmap.ftl confirms colored line drawing (7 colors, line width) and a label create/edit/delete dialog. medals.ftl confirms citation prompts, award recommendations, and a medals panel with approve/reject tabs. holocard.ftl gives exact treatment-status states (Surgery/Emergency/Permadead/Xeno Embryo) matching the 'flag treatment status' claim precisely. CMHealthIconsSystem.cs directly imports Content.Shared._RMC14.Medical.Unrevivable and computes DeadDefib/DeadClose/DeadAlmost/DeadDNR/Dead stages, confirming the multi-stage defib dependency the claim's skip_because cites. On graft_shape: all files I fetched (including CMHealthIconsSystem's imports) stay inside _RMC14 namespaces, so the seed's 'zero files outside fork namespace' is literally true, but the claim's 'entangled' verdict is about functional coupling to the fork's OTHER units (squad roster/ranks, hive structure, defib stage system), not about upstream file patching — and that coupling is directly visible in the code (CMHealthIconsSystem depends on RMCUnrevivableSystem; SquadTracker alerts are meaningless without the squad system; HiveTracker alerts are meaningless without hive leadership). That supports downgrading the seed's drop-in to entangled, exactly as the claim argues.

**serbia-strong**

*cosmetic · entangled · medium confidence · 8 forks carry it*

Players wearing the right eyewear see a much denser layer of floating icons above people's heads: a security HUD that distinguishes wanted, under surveillance, to-be-searched, paroled, incarcerated, demoted, discharged and marked-for-execution instead of just a couple of criminal flags, job badges for the fork's extra roles (magistrate, security pilot, Death Squad operative/commander/medic, RedWings pilots and medics) and faction badges that tag people as Nanotrasen, IOT or USSP combatants, medics or officers during faction-war events. **stellar-station**

*cosmetic · needs upstream edits · medium confidence · 8 forks carry it*

Instead of reading your condition from a handful of coarse icons, you get labelled sliding bars stacked around your character: a red Health bar that becomes a darker "Critical Health" bar once you go down and a grey "Dead" one when you die, a yellow Stamina bar for how much sprinting you have left, thin Hunger and Thirst bars on either side, a blue Respiration bar that drains as your air tank empties while internals are on, and a green Battery bar for anything that runs on a cell. Hovering a bar tells you plainly what it does ("If this gets to 0, you go critical", "The amount of gas left in the tank"). Alongside that, being incapacitated is spelled out in words rather than guessed at: a yellow "Stun Buildup" meter warns you that you are about to be knocked down and is already slowing you, and separate red "Stunned", "Slowed" and "Restrained" warnings tell you exactly why you cannot act, with "Restrained" also telling you that anyone dragging you will meet no resistance. Wielding a gun puts a crosshair on your cursor so you can see where you are actually aiming.

- **Take** — It turns the vaguest part of the game (how close am I to crit, how much air is left, why can't I move) into continuous labelled bars every player reads at a glance, and the bar itself is a data-driven prototype so a fork can add its own without touching C#.
- **Skip** — The resource bar files carry an SPDX header of `LicenseRef-Wallening`, a custom non-standard licence rather than MIT or AGPL, so grafting it into an AGPL fork is a licensing question before it is an engineering one; on top of that the Stunned / Stun Buildup / Restrained alerts are the readout for a fork stun-and-grab rework that does not exist upstream, so half the strings would have nothing to display.
- **Touches** — all-players, medical, security, silicons
- **Needs** — [close-quarters-combat-rework](combat.md#close-quarters-combat-rework)
- **Verified** — partly supported. Confirmed by direct inspection well beyond the cited evidence: the SPDX LicenseRef-Wallening header on resource_bars.yml and ResourceBarsComponent.cs is real. But the wiring is far more entangled than the claim's own reasoning suggests — Resources/Prototypes/Body/species_base.yml (the shared base every humanoid species inherits from) and Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Mobs/Cyborgs/base_borg_chassis.yml both carry inline `# Begin Stellar - Resource Bars` / `# End Stellar` edits that attach ResourceBars/MobThresholdResourceBars/SatiationResourceBars/InternalsResourceBar directly onto vanilla prototypes. Likewise Resources/Prototypes/Alerts/alerts.yml (vanilla, not under _ST/) has `# Begin Stellar changes` blocks reskinning the existing vanilla `Stun`, `Stamina`, `Handcuffed`, and `Ensnared` alert ids with new icons/text — these are NOT a novel fork-only 'stun-and-grab rework' as claimed, they are upstream SS14 alert mechanics being relabeled. So the claim's specific justification ('half the strings would have nothing to display' because the mechanic doesn't exist upstream) is not supported, but the graft_shape downgrade to needs-upstream-edits is still correct, for a different and stronger reason: this fork edits shared/vanilla files in place rather than overlaying only _ST-namespaced files.
  - unsupported: "separate red \"Stunned\", \"Slowed\" and \"Restrained\" warnings tell you exactly why you cannot act" — the cited status-effects.ftl does define `alerts-stellar-snared-name = [color=red]Slowed[/color]`, but that key is dead code: the only alert with a matching 'slowed' icon (id: Ensnared, in Resources/Prototypes/Alerts/alerts.yml) is wired to the plain vanilla keys `alerts-slowed-name`/`alerts-slowed-desc`, not to `alerts-stellar-snared-name`. So the in-game 'Slowed' alert is not confirmed to render red or use that custom description; the cited evidence string is simply unused.

### Admin and mentor tooling

<a id="admin-and-mentor-tooling"></a>

Extends staff tooling with extra panels and commands, separate mentor or curator help channels, event preference tracking, smites and honeypots.

**serbia-strong, rmc14, corvax, backmen**

*activity · entangled · medium confidence · 9 forks carry it*

Players get a second help channel next to ahelp: a mentor-help line staffed by experienced players rather than admins, with typing indicators, claim-tracking so two mentors do not answer at once, and its own chime and unread badge (and on one fork an AI assistant that drafts a first answer to your ahelp before a human picks it up). Staff get a much deeper toolbox: right-click a player to edit their traits (optionally handing over the matching gear), toggle status effects, change how many lives they have left, resize them, hand an antagonist a brand new objective, or swap the job icon floating over their head for a bespoke event faction icon. Bans get finer-grained too, so someone can be silenced in chat or barred from a specific species or antag role instead of being kicked off the server outright, and event runners get a props locker of admin-only kit: invisible walls and instant invisible portals, faction flags and capture pedestals for team-war events, and calibration knives that deal exactly one damage type on hit.

- **Take** — The mentor-help channel alone is a real quality-of-life win for a new-player-facing fork, and the finer-grained chat/species/role bans let staff correct one behaviour without removing a player from the round.
- **Skip** — It is four forks' staff toolkits piled into one bucket, most of it patches upstream ban managers, bwoink and the admin menu (chat and species bans even ship their own database migrations), and several of the shiniest pieces are welded to systems you would also have to take: the xeno-hive and marine-squad admin tabs, the bank-account commands, the TTS voice switcher.
- **Touches** — admin-staff, all-players, antagonist, ghost-roles
- **Needs** — [character-traits](roleplay.md#character-traits), [discord-and-external-services](misc.md#discord-and-external-services), [xenomorph-hive](antag.md#xenomorph-hive), [squads-ranks-and-skills](jobs.md#squads-ranks-and-skills)
- **Verified** — partly supported; assessed as needs upstream edits, corrected by the verifier. Every cited file I could fetch (admin-verbs.ftl, add-objective-window.ftl, job-icon-changer-window.ftl, backmen admin/toolshed.ftl, the Corvax invisible-wall/portal YAML, the SS220 debug-knife and capture-flag YAML, and both RMC14 mentor C# files) confirms the core mechanics the claim describes almost line-for-line: typing indicators (OnMentorHelpTypingUpdated/UpdateTypingIndicator), claim-tracking (_claims dict, ClaimButton, 'Claimed by'), unread badges and a chime sound (_unread, _unreadByPlayer, _mHelpSound), trait editing with an item-grant checkbox, status toggles, life counts, X/Y scale, an 'Add objective' dialog, a job-icon picker, invisible walls/portals, faction capture flags, and one-damage-type debug knives. However, browsing SerbiaStrong-220's tree showed the admin-verb piece lives in its own Content.Server/SS220/Administration/Systems/AdminVerbSystem.cs, a self-contained class that hooks in purely via SubscribeLocalEvent<GetVerbsEvent<Verb>> — a non-invasive extension point that needs zero upstream file edits to wire in, which undercuts a blanket 'needs-upstream-edits' read for that piece. I could not verify (GitHub API rate-limited) the skip_because's specific claim that chat/species bans patch the upstream ban manager and ship their own migrations, so I can't confirm fork-patches-upstream for that half of the bucket. Given the bucket mixes at least one genuinely drop-in-style piece (the verb menu, the mentor UIController hooks, the pure-YAML props) with unverified-but-plausible upstream-coupled pieces (bans/bwoink) and explicit cross-dependencies on other catalog units (TTS/Discord, xeno-hive and squad admin tabs), the honest verdict is that this is a heterogeneous 4-fork grab-bag rather than a single unit that uniformly needs upstream edits.
  - unsupported: on one fork an AI assistant that drafts a first answer to your ahelp before a human picks it up — no such feature exists in the cited RMC14 evidence (MentorHelpWindow.xaml.cs, StaffHelpUIController.cs). The only adjacent RMC14 directory, Content.Server/_RMC14/Mentor/ImaginaryFriend, implements an unrelated ghost-companion/advisor roleplay mechanic (a ghost becomes an invisible 'imaginary friend' following a living player), not an AI that drafts ahelp replies.
  - unsupported: Bans get finer-grained too, so someone can be silenced in chat or barred from a specific species or antag role instead of being kicked off the server outright (and the related skip_because detail that 'chat and species bans even ship their own database migrations') — none of the 12 cited evidence files mention bans, mutes, species restrictions, or database migrations at all; this rests on zero cited evidence.

**sunrise, rmc14, backmen**

*activity · needs upstream edits · medium confidence · 9 forks carry it*

Pressing the help key no longer goes straight to admins: players first pick "Admin Help" (rule-breaking, player reports, bugs) or "Mentor Help" (how do I do surgery, what does this machine do), so beginner questions reach a separate volunteer mentor team instead of clogging the admin queue. On Sunrise a mentor question becomes a proper ticket with a subject line, and mentors work a two-tab queue (Open / Closed) where they claim a ticket, reply, teleport to the player, unassign, or close it, with per-mentor stats for tickets and messages handled over a week, month, or all time. RMC-14 does the same thing as a live chat instead of a queue, with claim, teleport and typing indicators, plus a "De-Mentor" toggle so a mentor can step out and re-enter the channel. Backmen adds an optional GPT-backed assistant that can auto-answer ahelps when no admin picks them up, plus a pile of staff commands for editing a character's access, faction, sex, voice, bank balance, manifest record or fingerprints mid-round.

- **Take** — A dedicated mentor channel is the single highest-leverage retention tool for a small fork: new players get answers without an admin having to drop what they are doing, and the ticket queue plus per-mentor stats makes it a role trusted regulars can actually be given.
- **Skip** — It only pays off if you actually have people willing to sit as mentors — with no volunteers it is a second empty inbox — and it is not a clean drop-in: the ticket queue needs its own database storage, a new admin-permission flag, a lobby button, a keybind, and a rewrite of the existing help button to fork the choice between ahelp and mhelp.
- **Touches** — all-players, new-players, admin, mentor-staff
- **Verified** — supported. Every concrete gameplay claim checked out against fetched source: Sunrise's mentor-help-ui.ftl and MentorHelpWindow/NewTicketDialog XAML confirm the two-tab Open/Closed queue, ticket subject line, claim/reply/teleport/unassign/close actions, and week/month/all-time per-mentor stats; RMC-14's MentorWindow.xaml/StaffHelpWindow.xaml confirm the live-chat model with TypingIndicator, ClaimIndicator, Claim/Teleport buttons, De-Mentor toggle and a ReMentorButton, plus the Admin Help/Mentor Help choice screen; Backmen's GptAhelpSystem.cs plus CCVars.gpt.cs (including a gpt.no_admin_auto flag) confirm the optional GPT auto-responder, and toolshed.ftl plus AddBankAcсountCommand.cs/SetBankCurrencyCommand.cs confirm the full staff-command list (access, faction, sex/changesex, voice/changetts, bank balance, manifest/stationrecord, fingerprints). On graft shape, I fetched Content.Shared/Input/ContentKeyFunctions.cs from Sunrise directly and found a live "// Sunrise-Start" block adding OpenMentorHelp/OpenHelpChoice key functions inside this upstream (non-_Sunrise-namespaced) file, plus MentorHelp EF Core migrations sitting in Content.Server.Database/Content.Shared.Database (also outside the _Sunrise tree) — direct proof the fork's own copy edits upstream-shared files in place, not just that wiring it elsewhere would require edits.

**serbia-strong, goobstation, corvax**

*activity · needs upstream edits · medium confidence · 9 forks carry it*

This is the kit admins use to stage set-piece events, so what players actually see is a round that suddenly has scenery and rules the map never had: invisible walls fencing off an arena, invisible portals a staffer steps through, unseen drop pods that thump down carrying supplies or a strapped-in person, and flag postaments that two factions fight to plant their banner on for a war event. Staff can also flip your job status icon so the crew reads you as a different role, add or strip character traits and statuses mid-round, resize you, hand you a fresh objective, top up your remaining lives, equip an item straight onto a slot, or smite you with a "Thunderstrike". On the support side, adminhelp conversations are relayed out to a Discord bot that can answer back in-channel, and there are chat bans and species bans alongside the usual ban types.

- **Take** — Admin-run events are how a fork gets memorable rounds without shipping a whole gamemode, and these props (drop pods, invisible walls and portals, capture-point flags, job-icon swaps, trait and objective editors) are exactly the levers a staffer needs to improvise one live.
- **Skip** — Almost none of it is player-facing on its own, so it only pays off if you have active admins running events; the ban and AHelp-relay half also drags in database migrations and an external bot service you would have to host and maintain yourself.
- **Touches** — all-players, antagonist, ghost-roles, command

### PDA cartridge programs

Extra software for handheld computers: security watchlists, stock trading, crime assistants, banking apps, news readers and logistics dashboards.
