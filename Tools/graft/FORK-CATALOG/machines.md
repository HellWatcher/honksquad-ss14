### Access control rework

<a id="access-control-rework"></a>

Changes how doors and equipment check identity: rebuilt access sets, scan gates, secure terminals, keypad locks, grid ownership and ID-gated item use.

**rmc14**

*loop-change · entangled · medium confidence · 6 forks carry it*

Command staff work an ID terminal that first makes them badge in with their own card, then lets them slot a crewmate's card and re-badge it wholesale: pick a job, and the card instantly gets that role's entire door-and-locker set (UNMC Command, Medical, Engineering, Military Police, Requisitions, the marine squad roles, plus colony and corporate sets), tick individual accesses on top, post someone to a squad, flip their friend-or-foe tag, or "terminate" a card so it stops reading as friendly. Every one of those edits is written into the ship AI's public identification log, so a quiet promotion or a stripped card can be read back later by anyone who checks. Because access is bundled per role, your card is also what decides which equipment vendors will hand you gear, not just which doors open, and wall-mounted keycard stations need two people to swipe authorized cards at separate posts within a short window of each other before the ship will go to Red Alert.

- **Take** — Bundling access into named role groups turns ID editing from tag-by-tag clicking into one meaningful command decision, and the two-person keycard turn plus the public AI log give access changes real social weight.
- **Skip** — It is not a standalone access system: the console reaches into that fork's squad assignment, rank, skill, IFF faction and ship-AI logging systems, and the group tree itself is written around a marine job roster, so porting it means either dragging those in or rewriting most of the prototypes for your own jobs.
- **Touches** — command, security, medical, engineering, cargo, all-players
- **Needs** — [squads-ranks-and-skills](jobs.md#squads-ranks-and-skills), [extra-job-roster](jobs.md#extra-job-roster), [firearms-content-expansion](combat.md#firearms-content-expansion)
- **Verified** — supported. IdModificationConsoleSystem.cs directly imports and depends on SquadSystem, SharedRankSystem, SkillsSystem, GunIFFSystem, ARESCoreSystem, and Roles/OriginalRoleComponent — every mechanic the claim describes (job-group re-badge, squad assignment, IFF toggle, termination, AI logging) is implemented by calling into these fork-specific systems in the same file. The claim's own graft_shape ('entangled') is well supported and correctly overrides the seed's 'drop-in' path-data guess, which evidently could not see this C#-level coupling.
  - unsupported: "your card is also what decides which equipment vendors will hand you gear" — only indirectly evidenced (a single code comment in marine_access_groups.yml: "this exists because other squad members will have their own equipment vendors"); no vendor-gating system file was found/read to directly confirm access tags drive vendor permissions.

**serbia-strong**

*loop-change · needs upstream edits · medium confidence · 6 forks carry it*

Some doors only open if you approach them from the right side, so a checkpoint airlock lets people out freely but refuses anyone pushing in from the wrong direction, and certain doors can be gated on what you *are* rather than what your ID says, letting a door open for a borg or a specific faction while rejecting a stolen card. **rmc14**

*loop-change · entangled · high confidence · 15 forks carry it*

Instead of radio just working, players have to build and maintain the infrastructure that carries their voices. On the ground, comms towers and a sensor tower start broken: engineers weld the panels shut, cut and repair wiring, wrench the mast straight, restore power to the local APC, then tune the tower with an access tuner to add their side's radio channels — until that's done, nobody groundside can reach the ship, and the sensor tower feeds live enemy positions to the command map while it lasts (it randomly breaks again and needs re-repair). Where radio doesn't reach, players use a telephone network: rotary phones and wall phones are named after their location, you pick a destination off a tabbed list, the far phone audibly rings and emits a "starts ringing vigorously" emote, and whoever picks up hears you in a distinct purple anonymous whisper as long as you both keep the handset in hand and stay close.
