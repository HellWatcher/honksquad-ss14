### Announcer voice packs

<a id="announcer-voice-packs"></a>

Swappable voiced station announcers covering round start, alert levels, shuttle calls and round-end stingers, plus equipment hailer voice lines.

**corvax, ephemeral-space, frontier, gaby-station-now-dumont-station, goobstation, harmony-station, rmc14, serbia-strong, starlight, sunrise**

*cosmetic · needs upstream edits · high confidence · 7 forks carry it*

Instead of the usual text-only bulletins with a generic chime, the station gets a voiced announcer: a real human (or in-character AI) voice reads out round start, alert level changes, and event warnings like meteor showers, ion storms, power outages, outbreaks and alien sightings, and plays a stinger over the round-end screen. Several forks bolt extra flavour onto that, including joke alert levels with their own colour and voice clip, extra named alert states beyond the usual green-to-delta ladder, wall speakers that play announcements spatially so you hear them louder near a speaker and not at all in an unpowered section, and a security hailer button that makes an officer's helmet bark "HALT!", "FREEZE", "GET ON THE FLOOR" out loud to everyone nearby. The lines are typically recorded by a named community voice actor, so the announcer becomes part of the server's identity rather than generic sci-fi filler.

- **Take** — Cheap, high-visibility identity: a recognisable voice on every alert and event is the single most-heard thing on a server, and the bulk of it is audio files plus prototype overrides rather than new mechanics.
- **Skip** — The value is entirely in the recordings, which are the part you cannot copy for free — most packs are one specific voice actor under CC-BY with named attribution, so grafting the plumbing leaves you needing your own voice work, and the wiring still has to reach into upstream event, alert-level and round-end prototypes to swap the sounds.
- **Touches** — all-players, security, command
- **Verified** — partly supported. The claim's needs-upstream-edits downgrade from the drop-in seed is not just correct but understated: Starlight's Resources/Prototypes/AlertLevels/alert_levels.yml (the exact vanilla upstream path, no _Starlight prefix) is directly edited in place, with '# begin starlight'/'# end starlight' comment markers rewriting the sound field of the pre-existing green/blue/violet/yellow/red/gamma/delta/epsilon entries to point at _Starlight audio and appending new omega/theta/psi/orange levels — that is a real fork-patches-upstream file, contradicting the seed's zero-files-outside-namespace premise for this fork. Other forks in the bundle (Goob-Station's Content.Goobstation.Shared/Hailer/HailerComponent.cs, Sunrise's Content.Shared/_Sunrise/AnnouncementSpeaker/Components/AnnouncementSpeakerComponent.cs) are genuinely self-contained new files that would only need upstream wiring (equip slot / helmet-mask prototype, speaker placement) to integrate, matching the second reading. So both readings are simultaneously true across the bundled forks.
  - unsupported: "a security hailer button that makes an officer's helmet bark" — Goob-Station's HailerSystem.cs grants the hail action via GotEquippedEvent gated on SlotFlags.MASK, and the in-character line is sent with nameOverride: Name(uid) + "(SecMask)" — the item is a mask, not a helmet.
