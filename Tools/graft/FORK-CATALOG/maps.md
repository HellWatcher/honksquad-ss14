### Custom station maps and shuttles

<a id="custom-station-maps"></a>

The fork's own station rotation plus its centcomm, evacuation, cargo and event shuttles.

**corvax, cosmatic-drift, ephemeral-space, estacao-pirata, frontier, funky, gaby-station-now-dumont-station, goobstation, harmony-station, impstation, monolith, rmc14, serbia-strong, starlight, sunrise, viva-station, white-dream**

*structural · needs upstream edits · medium confidence · 6 forks carry it*

This is a content bucket, not a system: each fork's own roster of stations to play on, plus the ships that fly to and from them. Players get unfamiliar floor plans with their own department layouts, room names on the station map, and job slot counts baked in per station (Camp Chromite is a Nanotrasen re-education facility, Impstation ships a Doom-styled arena map for zero-job free-for-alls, Monolith's spawn points are USSP and Viper Group faction outposts with their own shipyards and armories). The shuttle half is a large library of alternates for the roles upstream already has one of: evac shuttles with different layouts, cargo and mining shuttles, CentComm stations, ERT and admin transports in engineering/medical/security/janitor/chaplain flavours, and event ships that fly in mid-round like clown patrols and alien tourists. Volume ranges from four files (Viva's two maps) to thousands (RMC-14's planetside operation maps and the Almayer).

- **Take** — A station map is the arena the whole round happens in, and each of these is a finished, playtested one — a fresh rotation is the single cheapest way to make a fork feel like its own server rather than a reskin of upstream.
- **Skip** — A map is only as portable as the entities placed on it: the plain ports (Estação Pirata's box/cluster/omega, Viva's two) drop in, but Frontier's, Monolith's, RMC-14's and White Dream's maps are built on their fork's shipyards, POI economy, area system and custom walls, so grafting one of those quietly drags a large fork system in behind it — and every map you adopt is a map you now own and must repair on every upstream tile, machine and job change.
- **Touches** — all-players, command, cargo, engineering, security, medical, service, antagonist
- **Verified** — partly supported. Core specifics check out: Viva's CampChromite.yml literally names the station "Nanotrasen Re-Education Facility, Camp Chromite"; Impstation's E1M1.yml is a 0-minPlayers StandardStationArena with Passenger [-1,-1] (zero-job arena, Doom-level-id naming); Viva really is exactly 4 files (2 prototype + 2 map data) for its 2 maps; Monolith's _Mono/PointsOfInterest/ussp.yml and _Mono/Shipyard/USSP + /Viper directories confirm faction outposts with their own ship rosters; Frontier's frontier.yml uses StandardFrontierStation with fork-only jobs (Contractor/Mercenary/Pilot), supporting the shipyard-economy entanglement claim. But the 'plain ports drop in' framing overclaims: Estação Pirata's actual map-pool wiring lives at the literal shared path Resources/Prototypes/Maps/Pools/default.yml (mixing its own map ids straight into the same file upstream uses for the default rotation, not a namespaced pool file like Frontier/Monolith use), and the cited evidence path for it (_ES/StationConfigs/default.yml) doesn't exist in that repo at all — the real namespace is _EstacaoPirata for code and unprefixed Maps/EstacaoPirata/ for the maps themselves. That's a genuine fork-patches-upstream case sitting inside what the claim calls the easy tier. The 'thousands' figure for RMC-14 was not independently verified (GitHub API rate-limited mid-check) though it's directionally plausible for that fork's scale.

### Ruins, points of interest and sector generation

<a id="ruins-poi-and-sector-generation"></a>

Off-station content the round is built around: salvage wrecks, space ruins, outposts and procedurally placed points of interest, plus chunk loading and cleanup for a persistent sector.

**frontier**

*structural · entangled · medium confidence · 4 forks carry it*

Instead of one station in empty space, the round opens on a whole populated sector: a disk-shaped asteroid belt with a central arrival outpost, then an inner ring of service stops (trade outpost, medical dispatch, lettered cargo depots), a middle ring dense with mineable rock and gas deposits, and an outer ring of icy asteroids and strange brass wrecks that researchers go hunting through. Which locations appear changes every round because each point of interest rolls its own spawn chance, distance from centre and minimum spacing from its neighbours, so crews fly out and find a different mix of shops, ruins and derelicts each shift, all listed as named warp points. Alongside that, salvagers can launch expeditions onto generated planets and station-sized dungeons (cave factory, virology lab, lava mercenary base, haunted site) with rolled weather, breathable-or-not atmosphere and hostile factions like carps, argocytes, dinosaurs or rogue silicons, and one-off bluespace encounters (a vault, a wizard tower, a Syndicate FTL intercept) can drop in as anomalies to go poke at.

- **Take** — It converts "the station" into "the sector": a round becomes an explorable map with varying destinations, which is the single biggest change you can make to what a shift feels like, and the spawn rules are data-driven so new locations are just another prototype plus a grid file.
- **Skip** — It only pays off if you also want the rest of the Frontier round shape — it wires into trade-crate destinations, cargo-depot and market-station CVARs, station deeds and warp/fax/holopad renaming, and it assumes players have ships to reach any of it, so on a single-station fork you would inherit the generator with nothing worth generating.
- **Touches** — all-players, cargo, salvage, science, command
- **Needs** — [shipyard-player-ships](cargo.md#shipyard-player-ships), [expedition-missions](cargo.md#expedition-missions), [cargo-bounties-and-market](cargo.md#cargo-bounties-and-market), [custom-station-maps](#custom-station-maps)
- **Verified** — supported. SectorTopology.xml confirms the disk-shaped belt, central Frontier Outpost, and the inner/middle/outer ring layout with trade outpost, medical dispatch, cargo depots, minerals/gas, icy asteroids and brass wrecks verbatim. PointOfInterestPrototype.cs/System.cs confirm per-POI SpawnChance, MinimumDistance/MaximumDistance, MinimumClearance, lettered cargo-depot naming, and warp-point renaming exactly as described. Directory listings for Resources/Maps/_NF/Dungeon (cave_factory, virology_lab, lava_mercenary, haunted) and Resources/Maps/_NF/Bluespace (vault, wizardtower, syndieftlintercept) confirm the specific named examples. expeditions.ftl and salvage-faction.ftl confirm rolled weather/atmosphere mods and hostile factions (carps, argocytes, dinosaurs, rogue silicons). The system code also hard-codes a dependency on Content.Server._NF.Trade's TradeCrateDestinationComponent and NFCCVars cargo/market CVars plus GameTicker preset-gating, which substantiates downgrading the seed's 'drop-in' guess to the claim's own 'entangled' assessment.

**serbia-strong**

*activity · needs upstream edits · medium confidence · 4 forks carry it*

Roughly five minutes into the round a gateway on the station finishes charging (poking it early just tells you it is still spooling up), then opens onto a randomly chosen off-station location that only exists for that round. Crew can walk through, explore the place, and come back through a return portal at the far end. The pool of destinations is a set of hand-built maps rather than generated terrain: a derelict shopping market, a scrapyard, a fuel station, a cargo outpost, an orbital penal colony, a chapel and engineering wreck, an arena, a wooded biodome and a small NT ship. Certain long-range guns are locked so they will only fire once you are actually standing on the dungeon map, so the expedition loadout cannot be turned on the station.

- **Take** — It is a large pile of finished, hand-made off-station locations plus a very small amount of glue code that drops a random one behind a portal each round, so you get a repeatable mid-round excursion without building a generator.

**sunrise**

*activity · needs upstream edits · medium confidence · 4 forks carry it*

Salvagers who fly out from the station find a much wider spread of places to break into: hand-built wrecks the magnet drags in (a defib wreck, a spider-clan hulk, xeno and science chunks, a merc sand camp, a syndicate outpost), fixed space ruins like an abandoned DJ station, a derelict flagship and an ancient whiteship, and procedurally assembled sites that are stitched together from room chunks so the same "Soviet station", "pirate base" or "crashed ship" never has quite the same floorplan twice. The generated sites come with their own furniture: plastitanium boundary walls, locked syndicate airlocks (a small fraction of entrances are rigged with a landmine), wall-mounted defib and extinguisher cabinets, cabling and corridor trim. There are also several off-station grids the round drops people onto directly, including nuclear operative staging planets (lava and western flavoured), a lavaland outpost, arenas and a transit hub, and the harder salvage difficulty tiers only start showing up after the round has been running a while rather than being available from minute one.

- **Take** — It is a large, mostly data-only pile of off-station destinations, and the room-atlas approach means four hand-drawn map files turn into many differently-shaped ruins instead of the same wreck every round.
- **Skip** — The maps only mean anything once they are wired into the salvage/expedition rules that spawn them, the C# piece is a partial extension of an upstream prototype class (so upstream has to be edited to accept it), and every one of these grids becomes yours to keep compiling against upstream map-format and entity-prototype churn.
- **Touches** — cargo, salvage, antagonist

**backmen, corvax, impstation, monolith, starlight, the-den**
